The Omnia Story

Everything begins with a vision.

Rooted in the Latin word meaning "all things," Omnia represents the art of bringing every element of music together.

Omnia Session Strings brings together artistry, personnel, technical expertise, and creative leadership for recording sessions, commissioned works, visual projects, and live productions.

From solo musicians and intimate ensembles to full orchestras, we coordinate the talent and resources needed to realize each musical vision.

We approach every project with musical sensitivity, preparation, collaboration, and a commitment to excellence. Our founders have earned four GRAMMY Awards collectively.

4× GRAMMY Award-Winning Founders · Four Collective Wins Hamilton Hardin & Nicole Hardin
Our Expertise

From concept to completion.

From a single musician to a full orchestra. From the recording studio to the concert stage. Omnia provides comprehensive musical services for artists, producers, churches, studios, venues, and creative teams.

Recording & Session Engineering

Professional studio recording, audio engineering, microphone placement, session preparation, multitrack recording, live orchestral tracking, and coordination with musicians and producers.

Musician Contracting & Ensemble Coordination

Personnel sourcing and contracting for tours, weddings, churches, recordings, concerts, special events, and productions. From solo instrumentalists to chamber ensembles and full symphony orchestras.

Strings & Ensemble Services

Soloists, duos, trios, quartets, string sections, chamber ensembles, and full orchestras for studio recording, live performance, filmed performances, and video shoots.

Orchestration, Arranging & Composition

Original music, custom arrangements, orchestration, score preparation, and musical development for recording artists, ensembles, orchestras, and live productions.

Music Production

Music producing, creative direction, pre-recording production, recording collaboration, mixing, mastering, and musical development from concept to final delivery.

Live Performances & Touring

Musical personnel and production support for churches, worship services, concert venues, theaters, tours, weddings, and special events.

Commissioned Works

Custom compositions, symphonic works, orchestral arrangements, and specially commissioned musical projects for organizations, ensembles, and artists.

Music Consulting

Guidance on instrumentation, ensemble selection, musician personnel, session logistics, musical planning, and orchestral production.
