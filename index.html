Our Story

Everything begins with a vision.

Derived from the Latin word meaning "all things," Omnia represents the art of bringing every element of music together.

Omnia Session Strings is a creative music company bringing together exceptional musicianship, thoughtful production, and artistic collaboration.

From intimate recording sessions to large-scale orchestral productions, we help artists, producers, composers, and creative teams bring their musical visions to life.

Our approach combines musical excellence, technical understanding, and the belief that extraordinary work happens through extraordinary collaboration.

Founded by Hamilton Hardin & Nicole Hardin
