Who We Are

The art of bringing everything together.

Founded by Hamilton Hardin and Nicole Hardin, Omnia Session Strings, LLC is a creative music company providing composition, orchestration, arranging, production, recording, musician contracting, and full-service session support.

Our name reflects our roots in strings, but our expertise reaches far beyond them. From intimate ensembles to complete orchestras, we help artists, producers, conductors, presenters, and organizations bring ambitious musical ideas to life.

Our founders have earned four GRAMMY Awards collectively, and their work spans orchestral institutions, recording artists, live productions, and broadcast stages.

What We Do

Our services.

Creative writing, production, musical personnel, and performance support—tailored to each project's artistic and practical needs.

Original Composition & Commissioned Works

Original compositions and custom commissions for orchestras, ensembles, artists, presenters, and special productions.

Full Orchestration & Symphonic Arranging

Orchestrations and full-orchestra arrangements for concerts, recordings, commissions, and featured artists.

String, Horn & Vocal Arranging

Tailored writing for string sections, brass and horn ensembles, vocals, and rhythm sections.

Scoring, Notation & Part Preparation

Conductor scores, score editing, instrumental parts, music engraving, and performance-ready materials.

Music Transcription

String, horn, piano, vocal, and other instrumental transcriptions for recording or live performance.

Recording & Session Strings

Professional string sessions, studio recording, live orchestral tracking, and coordination for filmed sessions.

Musician Contracting & Personnel

Sourcing and managing soloists, small ensembles, and full orchestras for recording, touring, events, and performances.

Music Production

Creative development, musical production, preproduction, tracking, and producer-artist collaboration.

Audio & Session Engineering

Studio recording, microphone and session preparation, multitrack capture, mixing, and mastering.

Live Performances & Touring

Instrumentalists and ensembles for concerts, tours, special events, and other live productions.

String Performance & Video Shoots

Live-performance visuals, filmed orchestral sessions, artist videos, and production coordination.

Music Consulting

Instrumentation, ensemble planning, artistic direction support, session logistics, and project strategy.
