The Omnia Story

Everything begins with a vision.

Rooted in the Latin word meaning "all things," Omnia represents the art of bringing every element of music together.

Omnia Session Strings is a creative music company specializing in recording, orchestration, production, musician contracting, and live performance.

We believe exceptional music is created when artistry, preparation, technical excellence, and collaboration come together with purpose.

From the first creative conversation to the final performance, we approach each project with musical sensitivity, attention to detail, and a commitment to bringing every vision to life.
